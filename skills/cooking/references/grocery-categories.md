# Grocery categories

The aisle taxonomy used to group grocery lists. `stores.md` in the kitchen
repo orders these per store; a category missing from a store's list goes at
the end.

| Category | Typical items |
|:--|:--|
| produce | vegetables, fruit, fresh herbs, garlic, onion, ginger, lemons, salad |
| bakery | bread, naan, tortillas, buns, pita |
| meat & seafood | chicken, beef, pork, lamb, sausage, fish, shrimp |
| dairy & eggs | milk, cream, yogurt, butter, cheese, eggs, tofu |
| dry goods & pasta | rice, pasta, oats, flour, lentils, dried beans, breadcrumbs, cereal |
| canned & jarred | canned tomatoes, beans, coconut milk, stock, broth, pasta sauce, olives, pickles, nut butters |
| spices & baking | dried spices, dried herbs, sugar, baking powder, vanilla, chocolate, nuts, seeds |
| international | soy sauce, fish sauce, curry paste, miso, gochujang, specialty noodles, tahini |
| frozen | frozen vegetables, frozen fruit, ice cream, frozen dumplings |
| beverages | juice, coffee, tea, sparkling water, wine, beer |
| household | foil, parchment, bags, paper towels, dish soap |

Rules of thumb:

- Oils and vinegars go in **canned & jarred** unless the store list has a
  dedicated slot.
- Fresh herbs are **produce**; dried are **spices & baking**.
- Anything in `pantry.md` is not listed under a category. It goes under the
  trailing **Already have** section instead.
- When unsure, pick the category where you would look first in the default
  store.
